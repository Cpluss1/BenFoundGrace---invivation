How I Met Her

A quiet beginning

Sometimes, the most beautiful chapter of our lives begins with an ordinary moment. What started as a simple greeting gradually became a friendship, and neither of us knew just how beautiful the journey ahead would be. Sometimes, God’s sweetest blessings begin in the most ordinary moments.

How I Met Him

A connection that grew

As time passed, friendship blossomed into something deeper. Through conversations, laughter, shared moments and getting to know each other, we discovered a connection worth nurturing. What began as friendship became a beautiful love story, guided by faith, patience and God’s perfect timing.
